Collaborator directory updated for the autumn 2026 cycle

Posted by ORION Communications · 6 Oct 2026, 09:15 IST

Autumn 2026 MIRROR collaborators announced

Posted by ORION Communications · 18 Aug 2026, 10:05 IST

ORION Research Systems is pleased to welcome the autumn 2026 cohort of partner collaborators to MIRROR, its survey data anonymization and research archive project.

Among the incoming collaborators from BITS Pilani is Navya Mulay (Senior Researcher, Department of Computer Science and Information Systems), who joins MIRROR under the supervision of Prof. Archisman Joshi. Collaborators will contribute to the methodology, documentation and review workstreams.

Onboarding sessions will run through the end of August. Dashboard and repository access will be issued to each collaborator individually upon completion of onboarding.
